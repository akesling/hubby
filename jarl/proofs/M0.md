# M0: claim, subject and admissible environment

Governing requirements: CORRECTNESS_PLAN.md sections 1–5 and M0. This record
specifies the proof subject; it does not certify implementation correctness.
M0 completion is supported by the exit-criterion audit below. M1–M8 remain open.

## Claim scope

The target remains conditional total correctness for both `Node<V,S,N,CAP>` and
`Cluster<V,S,MAX,CAP>`. Safety quantifies over all admissible finite histories,
including arbitrary legal capacities, arbitrary runtime voter/learner sets,
sequential reconfiguration, crashes, snapshots, errors and resource exhaustion.
Liveness quantifies over selected admitted/retried operations with sufficient
remaining counters/capacity and the noncircular scheduling premises in section 10
of the plan. Safety does not assume those progress premises.

`MAX` bounds simultaneous membership slots, not lifetime identities. `CAP` may
change through growth; `MAX` currently does not. Fixed `Node` accepts positive
capacity, including capacities too small for progress. Dynamic `Cluster` requires
at least four slots. A progress witness with spare capacity does not narrow the
safety claim to such capacities. Restored state is admitted by provenance from
a durable execution, not merely by `State::restore` accepting its shape.

Jarl remains readable, no_std, sans-I/O and allocation-/unsafe-free internally.
External payload allocation and Clone/Drop behavior are explicit consumer
boundaries. Provium is a dev-dependency and reusable provider. No runtime proof
instrumentation, executor, new toolchain or non-registry backend is required.

## Subject accounting

`coverage.json` contains every syntactically inventoried production item and its
source hash, complete line/column range, unresolved call spellings, requirement IDs and review status.
Named review contexts associate every item with all declared build profiles,
A01–A10 review duties and concrete unresolved limitations. Known public roots
account for themselves; unknown private-to-root associations are explicitly
unresolved. Contract references are checked against project obligation manifests,
not treated as proof certificates. `source_coverage_review_is_current` recomputes
this inventory. It catches changed
source, methods, variants, fields and late effects. It does not resolve Rust
names, macro expansion, trait dispatch or reachable closure. Those limits remain
recorded in the inventory and are P01/P03/P05/P10 implementation obligations.
The complete-proof gate must continue to reject until closure and source
preservation are established, regardless of any review status assigned here.

Conservatively including an item is not evidence that it is reachable. Conversely,
private methods and derived/implicit operations cannot be omitted because they are
not public roots. The ledger reviews 187 items and 101 conservative public roots, including public
types and explicit trait implementations. Derived operations remain associated
with their hashed source type under C12; their expansion is not claimed resolved.
The ledger's call spellings are unresolved review inputs, not
proved edges. Complete root-to-item/callee resolution remains explicitly open.

Test explorers and `examples/support/*journal.rs` are separate evidence subjects.
They do not become part of the core proof by being used in tests. Filesystem
encoding, locking, corruption and flush ordering need their own refinement
before an end-to-end filesystem deployment is claimed verified.

No assumption asserts election uniqueness, valid generated outputs, quorum
intersection across arbitrary configurations, leader completeness, or commitment
soundness. Those are implementation obligations. A03/A05/A08 constrain the host;
they do not assume that Jarl emits the correct transaction or waits before output.

## Formal event and witness model

`specification/Model.lean` is checked through Provium's explicitly labelled
`specification_only` project. It declares bounded lifetime IDs/counters, runtime
membership with old/new/learner sets, immutable genesis, per-owner capacities and
timers, volatile/durable/pending state, application exposure, emissions and a
network of emission identifiers. A finite list represents an arbitrary finite
population, never a fixed three-node universe.

The event alphabet separates API input, internal execution, callback return and
panic, unwind Drop, abort, save initiation/Pending/durable completion/acknowledgment,
error, cancellation, crash/restart, materialization, delivery/drop/replay,
committed/snapshot exposure, actual application, provisioning, growth and ticks.
This prevents declaring every public method atomic by construction. Exact Rust
microsteps, message variant semantics and protocol transitions are still to be
derived in M1–M5; this model is not offered as their replacement.

Checked witness interfaces:

- `genesis_witness`: any admissible genesis/population/capacities/settings yields
  an initial world; `genesis_owners` preserves exactly the supplied owner IDs.
- `concrete_genesis_witness`: the admissibility premises have a concrete witness
  for either engine; no safety theorem is obtained by making genesis impossible.
- `save_witness` and `pending_witness`: arbitrary exact transactions can complete
  and be acknowledged, including an asynchronous wait.
- `ambiguous_crash_witness`: durability can precede a crash without acknowledgment,
  and recovery retains the completed checkpoint.
- `cancellation_witness`: a quiescent canceled save can be retried successfully.
- `ack_requires_durable` and `crash_preserves_durable`: the host contract's event
  boundary facts hold in this serial witness model.

The native witnesses are tests/host.rs, tests/dynamic_host.rs and
tests/async_host.rs. They exercise fixed and dynamic hosts, successful saves,
recovery, pending futures, cancellation and errors with actual Jarl operations.
Their storage/application/provisioning choices supply concrete contracts for the
finite runs. They do not establish an infinite progress schedule or a source
refinement theorem for the filesystem backend.

## Theorem statement review

The M0 model exposes a `Refinement` interface requiring initial-state simulation,
event simulation and equality of observations. It is a structure whose proof
fields must be supplied, not an axiom declaring that Jarl implements it. Error,
panic and output-order observations belong in that interface. The root targets
remain the exact R01–R15, S01–S16 and L01–L07 statements in the plan.

Self-review findings incorporated here:

1. Fixed and dynamic engines have different minimum capacities; do not silently
   restrict fixed-engine safety to dynamic-engine capacity requirements.
2. Different owners may use different timers/seeds and capacities.
3. Old voters can simultaneously be target learners during demotion; forbidding
   that overlap would exclude legitimate joint configurations.
4. A successful durable save followed by cancellation/error/crash must not revert
   storage. Pending and acknowledgment are not synonymous with durability.
5. Genesis satisfiability and host-step witnesses prove model consistency only;
   full source refinement and noncircular temporal premises remain required.

This is implementation self-review, not the independent review required by V10.
Specification revisions must update this review and corresponding negative checks.

The model project checks 18 obligations. Every theorem's transitive axioms are
restricted to the existing approved logical basis. Provium labels its certificate
`specification_only` with `source_correspondence_proved=false` and
`whole_program_proved=false`. Native host evidence remains explicitly distinct.

The reviewed-input hashes are deliberate change detectors. Editing the model,
plan, scope, build matrix or M0 gate requires reviewing the affected statements
and refreshing m0-review.json; the gate does not update that review automatically.
Neither a hash match nor this M0 audit establishes Rust semantic preservation.

Remaining work belongs to the original later milestones: resolved expanded
source and effective compiler invocation closure (P01/P03/P05), ownership and
outcome semantics (P04/P07/P08), source-preservation and composed correctness
roots (P09–P12), complete protocol/host refinement (M2–M6), conditional progress
(M7), and release replay/independent review (M8). The strongest implementation
proof level remains component-checked.
