The new capture command is reusable Provium infrastructure. It runs only installed
Rust tools and locked/offline Cargo; it does not install targets or dependencies.
Only Cargo creates or writes the fresh target tree. Wrapper code, argument
records, source snapshots and reports stay under consumer evidence directories.
Unsupported Cargo configuration and existing wrapper overrides are rejected
explicitly. The report sets `semantic_preservation_proved=false` and is not a
proof certificate. Captured builds do not close P01 by themselves.

Membership iteration now has complete source-derived voter, old-voter and learner
projections, with exact ordered sequence and origin contracts. Composition proves
that finalization preserves voters and learners and removes all old voters for
arbitrary slot counts. This advances P02/P03 and C03; R01 still needs constructor,
identity, configuration and caller invariants, and P04/P10 source refinement
remains open.

The complete `contains` and `is_voter` bodies now have identity-query contracts:
`is_voter` agrees with membership in the current or old voter sequence, and every
voter participates. Their reusable structural equality interpretation is explicit;
source-value refinement and caller validation are still required.

`Membership::include` now retains its complete existing-identity-first search,
empty-slot fallback, initialization and tag dispatch. Contracts prove bounds for
the selected index, exact full-array failure, capacity and other-slot preservation,
the returned identity, exact record effects, and preservation of identity uniqueness.
Complete configuration validation still requires the constructor contracts below;
this is component evidence.

The complete `Membership::restore` loops and `new` forwarding body now compose
with `include`. Successful construction preserves capacity and establishes unique
identities from empty slots; necessary input conditions prove nonempty voters,
distinct inputs and learner/target-voter disjointness. Exact output sets and identity
union are checked. Acceptance holds exactly when those input conditions hold and
the distinct identity union fits capacity; every rejection is Config. Stable
construction yields no old voters and is not joint. Reachable callers and remaining
membership transformations/quorum helpers remain open for R01, as does
source/ownership/index/equality refinement.

`Membership::peers` now has a complete source-derived array/Option field map.
Its contracts preserve every slot position and empty slot, preserve capacity,
and connect returned identities to successful constructor inputs. This closes
its component translation under C03, not peer reconciliation or stale-response
identity binding. Rust Copy/array/Option ownership refinement remains open.

## M2: first global election proof

M2 follows M1; it is not closed by the existing election-deadline component.
Required implementation roots include complete fixed-engine initialization,
`tick`, `step`, campaign/pre-vote/vote handling, `follow`, `become_leader`,
send/reply/materialization and persistence/recovery. Other reachable API actions
need their election-state frame/outcome contracts as well.

The proof must establish R08/R09 and S01/S02 by induction from admissible
initialization over arbitrary histories, including crashes between durable save
and acknowledgment. It must derive durable one-vote-per-term and term monotonicity
from the generated transitions, bind granted replies to persisted votes, discharge
quorum intersection/counting hypotheses, and derive historical leader uniqueness.
No assumption may state those conclusions. Any remaining source-translation trust
must be explicit; M6 remains the full correspondence closure milestone.

No global election theorem has been established yet.
